THE SEARCH FOR LIFE

WHAT DO WE LOOK FOR?

When scientists investigate whether a world could potentially support life, they consider many physical and chemical conditions. Click each one.

01

LIQUID WATER

+
Liquid water is considered important because life as we know it depends on water. Scientists therefore look for environments where liquid water could exist.
02

CHEMICAL INGREDIENTS

+
Scientists investigate the presence of elements and molecules that could take part in biological chemistry.
03

ENERGY

+
Life needs energy. Scientists therefore consider possible energy sources available in different environments.
04

STABILITY

+
A potentially habitable environment would need suitable conditions to remain available for long enough for complex chemistry to develop.
