WORLDS THAT MAKE US WONDER

WHERE MIGHT
WE LOOK?

OCEAN WORLDS

Moons such as Europa and Enceladus show evidence suggesting subsurface oceans. Water makes them scientifically fascinating places to investigate.

EXOPLANETS

Thousands of planets have been discovered orbiting stars beyond our Sun. Some exist in regions where conditions could potentially be suitable for life.

EXTREME WORLDS

Life on Earth exists in surprisingly challenging environments, reminding scientists that habitability may be more diverse than once imagined.

THE SEARCH

WHAT DO
WE LOOK FOR?

01 — LIQUID WATER+
Liquid water is considered an important ingredient for life as we understand it, making environments with possible liquid water especially interesting.
02 — CHEMICAL INGREDIENTS+
Scientists look for elements and molecules that could participate in chemistry associated with life.
03 — ENERGY+
Life needs energy. Scientists investigate possible energy sources, from sunlight to chemical reactions.
04 — STABILITY+
A potentially habitable environment may need conditions that remain suitable long enough for complex chemistry to occur.
