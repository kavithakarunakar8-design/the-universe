THE SEARCH FOR LIFE

ARE WE ALONE?

Somewhere among the countless stars may be worlds with conditions very different from our own. Scientists are searching for clues that could help us understand whether life exists beyond Earth.

WHAT WOULD LIFE
LOOK LIKE ELSEWHERE?

We have only one confirmed example of life: Earth. That makes the search for life beyond our planet one of science's greatest questions.

🪐

EXOPLANETS

Exoplanets are planets that orbit stars outside our Solar System. Thousands have been confirmed, showing that planets are common throughout our galaxy.

🌍

HABITABLE WORLDS

Scientists study worlds where conditions might allow liquid water to exist. This region is often called the habitable zone.

🧬

INGREDIENTS FOR LIFE

Water, suitable chemistry and a stable environment are among the factors scientists investigate when considering potential habitability.

🔭

SPACE TELESCOPES

Powerful telescopes can study distant stars and planets and sometimes analyse the light passing through an exoplanet's atmosphere.

A WORLD WE CANNOT VISIT

Imagine discovering a planet many light-years away. We may never physically reach it, yet the light arriving from that distant world can tell scientists remarkable things about it.

THE INGREDIENTS OF POSSIBILITY

LIQUID WATER An important ingredient for life as we know it.
CHEMISTRY Complex chemistry plays a central role in life.
ENERGY Living systems need sources of usable energy.
• • •

LISTENING TO THE COSMOS

Scientists search the sky for signals and other evidence that could reveal information about distant worlds. So far, no confirmed evidence of extraterrestrial life has been found.
