CHAPTER 02 • THE UNKNOWN

WHAT WE
DO NOT KNOW

Some of the biggest mysteries in modern science are hidden in plain sight. We can observe their effects, but their true nature remains uncertain.

COSMIC MYSTERY ARCHIVE

THREE GREAT
QUESTIONS.

FILE 01

BLACK HOLES

Regions of spacetime where gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon.

Scientists study black holes through their effects on nearby matter, light and spacetime. They remain among the most extreme objects known.

FILE 02

DARK MATTER

An unseen form of matter inferred from its gravitational effects on galaxies and other cosmic structures.

It does not appear to interact strongly with light, making it difficult to observe directly. Its exact nature remains unknown.

FILE 03

DARK ENERGY

A name given to whatever is associated with the accelerated expansion of the universe.

Scientists know that cosmic expansion is accelerating, but the underlying cause is still one of modern cosmology's major questions.
